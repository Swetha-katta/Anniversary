A Letter For You

FROM MY HEART TO YOURS

Dear Satya Prakash,

You are the best husband I could ever ask for. You are very calm and quiet, and I truly love the peace and comfort you bring into my life.

You never easily say no to me. Whatever I ask for, you always try your best to give it to me. These little things may look simple, but they mean so much to me.

From the day we first met in Mogallamuru, I never imagined how beautiful our journey together would become.

And then came our journey to Dubai. Taking my first flight with you is one memory that will always be special to me. Experiencing something new with you made it even more beautiful.

Thank you for always being there for me. Thank you for your patience, your calm nature, your care, and for all the little things you do that make my life happier.

I may not always say everything that is in my heart, but I want you to know that I appreciate you more than words can explain.

I hope we continue to travel together, laugh together, make memories together, and grow old together.

No matter where life takes us, I always want you by my side.

With all my love,
Your Swetha ❤️
